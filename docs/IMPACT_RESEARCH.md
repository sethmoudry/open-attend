## 1. Documentation Burden

### The "2:1 Ratio" -- For every 1 hour of patient care, 2 hours on EHR/desk work

- **Source:** Sinsky C, Colligan L, Li L, et al. "Allocation of Physician Time in Ambulatory Practice: A Time and Motion Study in 4 Specialties." *Annals of Internal Medicine*. 2016;165(11):753-760.
- **URL:** https://www.acpjournals.org/doi/10.7326/M16-0961
- **Key numbers:**
  - Physicians spend **27.0% of their day on direct patient care** and **49.2% on EHR/desk work**
  - Even *inside* the exam room: **37% of time** is spent on EHR, only **52.9%** on the patient
  - **1-2 hours of after-hours "pajama time"** each night on EHR tasks
- **Study details:** 57 U.S. physicians across family medicine, internal medicine, cardiology, and orthopedics; 430 hours of direct observation

### 16 Minutes of EHR Time Per Encounter

- **Source:** Tai-Seale M, Olson CW, Li J, et al. "Physician Time Spent Using the Electronic Health Record During Outpatient Encounters." *Annals of Internal Medicine*. 2020;172(3):169-174.
- **URL:** https://www.acpjournals.org/doi/10.7326/M18-3684
- **Key numbers:**
  - Average **16 minutes 14 seconds** of EHR time per encounter
  - Chart review (33%), documentation (24%), ordering (17%)
- **Study details:** ~100 million encounters, ~155,000 physicians, 417 health systems

### "Pajama Time" -- After-Hours Documentation

- **Source:** AMA analysis of EHR log data; Arndt BG et al. "Tethered to the EHR." *Annals of Family Medicine*. 2017;15(5):419-426.
- **URL (AMA summary):** https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/digital-health/family-doctors-spend-86-minutes-pajama-time-ehrs-nightly
- **Key numbers:**
  - Family physicians spend **86 minutes per night** on after-hours EHR work
  - **20.9% of physicians** spend >8 hours/week on EHR outside clinic hours (as of 2024)
  - Pajama time independently associated with **emotional exhaustion and intent to leave practice**

## 2. Physician Burnout

### Bureaucratic Tasks = #1 Cause of Burnout

- **Source:** Medscape Physician Burnout & Depression Report, 2024.
- **URL:** https://www.medscape.com/sites/public/lifestyle/2024
- **Key numbers:**
  - **62% of burned-out physicians** cite bureaucratic/clerical tasks as the primary cause
  - **49% of physicians** reported burnout in 2024 (down from 53% in 2023)
  - Female physicians: **56% burnout** vs. male: **46%**

### AMA National Survey

- **Source:** AMA Organizational Biopsy, 2024. "U.S. Physician Burnout Hits Lowest Rate Since COVID-19."
- **URL:** https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/physician-health/us-physician-burnout-hits-lowest-rate-covid-19
- **Key numbers:**
  - **43.2% burnout rate** in 2024 (down from 48.2% in 2023, 53% in 2022)
  - Nearly **18,000 physician responses** across 43 states
  - >1/3 cite **ineffective EHR systems and documentation requirements** as taking away from patient care

## 3. Medical Scribe Costs

### Human Scribe Salaries

- **Source:** Salary.com, Medical Scribe Salary Data (February 2026); BLS Occupational Data.
- **URL:** https://www.salary.com/research/salary/benchmark/medical-scribe-salary
- **Key numbers:**
  - Average salary: **$41,129/year** (range: $29,000-$50,000+)

## 4. Documentation Errors & Patient Safety

### 20% of Malpractice Cases Involve Documentation Failures

- **Source:** Candello (CRICO). "For the Record: The Effect of Documentation on Defensibility and Patient Safety." November 2024.
- **URL:** https://www.candello.com/Insights/Candello-Reports/For-the-Record-Documentation
- **Key numbers:**
  - **20% of 65,000+ malpractice cases** (2014-2023) involved at least one documentation failure
  - Documentation issues **more than double** the odds of indemnity payment
  - **Illegible documentation** had the highest odds ratio (3.8x) of closing with payment
  - **36% of documentation errors** stem from copy-paste
- **Database:** Candello contains ~1/3 of all U.S. medical malpractice claims

### Medical Errors as a Leading Cause of Death

- **Source:** Makary MA, Daniel M. "Medical error -- the third leading cause of death in the US." *BMJ*. 2016;353:i2139.
- **URL:** https://hub.jhu.edu/2016/05/03/medical-errors-third-leading-cause-of-death/
- **Key numbers:**
  - **>250,000 deaths/year** in the U.S. attributed to medical errors
  - Third leading cause of death behind heart disease (~600K) and cancer (~600K)

## 6. Time Savings from AI Scribes

### Nuance DAX Cohort Study

- **Source:** Tierney AA, Gayre G, et al. "The Impact of Nuance DAX Ambient Listening AI Documentation: A Cohort Study." *JAMA Network Open*. 2024.
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10990544/
- **Key numbers:**
  - **20.4% reduction** in note documentation time
  - **9.3% improvement** in same-day note closure
  - **30% reduction** in after-hours EHR work

### Burnout Reduction with Ambient AI Scribes

- **Source:** Nishimura A, et al. "Use of Ambient AI Scribes to Reduce Administrative Burden and Professional Burnout." *JAMA Network Open*. 2025.
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12492056/
- **Key numbers:**
  - Burnout dropped from **51.9% to 38.8%** (13.1 percentage point reduction) after 30 days
  - Study: 263 physicians across 6 health systems
  - Mass General Brigham reported **21.2% absolute reduction** in burnout within 84 days

### UCLA Randomized Clinical Trial

- **Source:** UCLA Health. "UCLA Study Finds AI Scribes May Reduce Documentation Time and Improve Physician Well-Being." 2025.
- **URL:** https://www.uclahealth.org/news/release/ucla-study-finds-ai-scribes-may-reduce-documentation-time
- **Key numbers:**
  - **~7% improvement in burnout scores** vs. control group
  - 238 physicians randomized across DAX Copilot, Nabla, and control

### Kaiser Permanente Scale

- **Source:** JMIR AI Rapid Review on Digital Scribes. 2025.
- **URL:** https://ai.jmir.org/2025/1/e76743
- **Key numbers:**
  - **15,791 hours of documentation time saved** in one year
  - Across **2.57 million encounters**
  - Physicians report saving **2-3 hours daily** and seeing **15% more patients**

### Industry Adoption

- **Source:** Various industry reports, 2024-2025.
- **Key numbers:**
  - Abridge: **50,000 doctors** across **50 health systems** by late 2024
  - Microsoft/Nuance DAX Copilot: deployed across major health systems nationwide
  - Market moving rapidly from pilot to enterprise-wide deployment
